Vice Cover turns your own photos and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel poster cover art — sunset sky, retro sun, skyline and palms, bold caption lettering — and exports it as a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. It runs fully offline in the browser; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — load up to three photos (or use the samples), pick a panel layout, grade and lettering style, crop each panel by clicking it, and export any of the three post sizes.
